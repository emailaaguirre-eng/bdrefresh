2. Emergency Definition

An emergency is a production outage, severe business interruption, active misuse, or similarly urgent event accepted by B&D for priority handling. Urgency alone does not guarantee immediate availability.

3. Time and Expenses

Billable time includes investigation, communication, backups, coordination, implementation, testing, documentation, and restoration. Third-party fees and travel are additional when approved or reasonably necessary.

4. No Guaranteed Resolution

B&D will use commercially reasonable efforts but does not guarantee that every incident can be resolved, particularly when caused by unsupported systems, third parties, missing access, compromise, or damaged data.
